Third-party artwork in this folder.

rn-cover.png is this post's cover, the package's style entry points drawn
for it, and shows only Keyline Icons.

logos/ holds each project's own mark, used only to name it in the comparison
table and on this post's cover, and each remains its owner's trademark:
expo.svg is the mark from the expo.dev header without its wordmark, filled
black; react-native-vector-icons.svg is the React atom that heads the
react-native-vector-icons README, drawn from react.dev's own logo;
lucide.svg is lucide.dev/logo.light.svg; tabler.svg the mark from the
tabler.io header without its wordmark; and phosphor.svg the phosphor-logo
icon from @phosphor-icons/core (MIT, notice below), filled black.

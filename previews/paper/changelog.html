Initial release

Version 0.1.0 · 20 Aug 2026

The first cut of the set: 530 drawings on one 24 × 24 grid, at a 2px keyline, built for shadcn/ui and free under the MIT licence. Every icon is drawn in stroke (530), duotone (440) and fill (392), and ships as an SVG, a JSX snippet and a React component.

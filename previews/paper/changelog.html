Initial release

The first cut of the set: 503 drawings on one 24 × 24 grid, at a 2px keyline, built for shadcn/ui and free under the MIT licence. Every icon is drawn in stroke (503), duotone (415) and fill (368), and ships as an SVG, a JSX snippet and a React component.

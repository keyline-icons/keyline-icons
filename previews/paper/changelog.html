0.1.1

Initial release · 23 Aug 2026

The first cut of the set: 527 drawings on one 24 × 24 grid, at a 2px keyline, built for shadcn/ui and free under the MIT licence. Every icon is drawn in stroke (527), duotone (437) and fill (389), and ships as an SVG, a JSX snippet and a React component.
